About the New Nomad Institute

New Nomad Institute (NNI) is a not-for-profit research and action organization dedicated to integrating nomadic and indigenous knowledge in addressing contemporary climate, social, and technological issues. Rooted in Mongolia's living nomadic traditions, NNI works at the intersection of research, education, and cultural exchange to cocreate resilient and sustainable futures.

Our work is developed collaboratively with herders, artists, filmmakers, writers, and researchers, aiming to shift dominant narratives and position indigenous knowledge as a critical resource for addressing global challenges.
